# Validation history

This is a concise record of observed outcomes. Detailed hypotheses, raw diagnostic interpretation, and rejected approaches remain in the linked SPIKE records.

Unless an entry says otherwise, the recorded environment is Project Zomboid Build 42.20.x on a dedicated server with one connected test client.

| Date | Version | Scope | Outcome | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-08-30 | 0.0.24 | Dedicated-server crowd combat | Passed the SPIKE-001 feasibility closeout: synchronized pursuit, validated damage, and lethal corpse handling worked without a reported Zombie Factions exception in the recorded run. | [SPIKE-001](spikes/SPIKE-001-zombie-targeting-and-combat-feasibility.md) |
| 2026-08-30 | 0.0.31 | Standing faction bite presentation | Failed final stress validation. Readable bites and validated damage were observed, but native `AttackState` attempted to cast an `IsoZombie` target to `IsoPlayer`, crashed the client, and forced a disconnect. | [SPIKE-003](spikes/SPIKE-003-synchronized-combat-presentation.md) |
| 2026-08-30 | 0.0.32 | No-native-target standing combat | Partially passed. No faction exception or crash was observed; `impactExactTarget=0` and `nativeZombieTargetsCleared=0`, while crowd collisions produced validated damage and occasional reactions. An isolated pair repeatedly animated with zero collisions or damage until additional zombies forced contact. Sound was absent, reaction variety was limited, and the Horde Spawner appeared to require a second click. | [SPIKE-003](spikes/SPIKE-003-synchronized-combat-presentation.md) |
| 2026-08-30 | 0.0.33 | Horde Spawner control reuse | Failed immediate UI validation. After replacing the proven independent post-resize controls with the vanilla anchored controls, the bottom control area rendered empty and prevented the planned combat retest. | [Issue #5](https://github.com/jonathanjacobs/pz-zombie-factions/issues/5) |
| 2026-08-30 | 0.0.34 | Standing combat and Horde Spawner regression | Passed the controlled dedicated-server, one-client standing scope. The isolated opening pair produced collision-driven requests, sounds, and accepted damage before reinforcements. The full run recorded 218 collisions and sounds, 132 accepted damage acknowledgments, 17 armed reactions, zero exact native targets, zero native-target clears, and no faction error or prior crash signature. Four client spawn successes matched four server spawn events, and direct observation confirmed visible controls and successful first-click behavior. Two-client separation and crawlers were not exercised; 81 distance rejections remain tracked by Issue #1. | [SPIKE-003](spikes/SPIKE-003-synchronized-combat-presentation.md), [Issue #5](https://github.com/jonathanjacobs/pz-zombie-factions/issues/5) |
| 2026-08-31 | 0.0.35 | Configurable combat-distance envelope | Accepted `0.80` client and `1.60` server as the default distance gates based on operator testing. The supplied paired logs recorded `0.80`/`1.80`, not `0.80`/`1.60`; they confirmed stable configuration propagation (`damageConfigMismatch=0`), sustained collision-driven combat, and no Zombie Factions exception, but are not direct log validation of the final server value. All existing server authority checks remain active. | [Issue #1](https://github.com/jonathanjacobs/pz-zombie-factions/issues/1) |
