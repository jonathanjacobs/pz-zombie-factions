| 2026-09-06 | 0.0.41 | Sprinter-versus-sprinter close-range convergence failure | Reproduced [#10](https://github.com/jonathanjacobs/pz-zombie-factions/issues/10) directly and at higher severity than the shambler case that first recorded it. The first isolated sprinter pair sustained 3.31–3.48 tiles/second across three consecutive summary windows with roughly 95 travel samples each, then dropped to 0.017 tiles/second over 24 samples while still holding sprint intent for a full 10 seconds. The operator observed the pair repeatedly overshooting and circling each other before one connected by chance, and combat only resumed after a third zombie was spawned 26 seconds later. Arithmetic accounts for it: at 10 Hz controller passes a sprinter advances 0.33–0.35 tiles per pass and a converging pair closes 0.66–0.70, while the whole 1.20-to-0.50 engagement band is 0.70 tiles wide and melee authorization requires a pass observing 0.65 or nearer. A converging sprinter pair can therefore cross the entire band between two passes, where a shambler at 0.05 tiles per pass gets roughly fourteen observations inside it. Sprint intent is currently only cleared on reaching contact distance, so there is no deceleration phase at all. | [Issue #10](https://github.com/jonathanjacobs/pz-zombie-factions/issues/10) |
